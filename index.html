Travel smart

A little local knowledge
goes a long way.

Sri Lanka is wonderfully welcoming. These simple checks help you avoid the occasional tourist trap and enjoy it with peace of mind.

!
Common pressure tactic

“Your hotel is closed.”

A driver may suggest your chosen place is unavailable and offer another. Call your hotel directly before changing plans.

Verify independently, every time.
01

Use official ticket counters

At attractions and stations, buy from clearly marked counters—not helpful strangers nearby.

02

Check the route before you go

Keep your map open and agree on stops, tolls, and the final fare before a taxi journey.

03

Keep valuables out of sight

Use hotel safes where available and take normal care in crowded markets, buses, and trains.

04

Ask before photographing people

A warm gesture of respect goes a long way, especially at temples and in rural communities.
